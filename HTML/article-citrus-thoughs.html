Citrus Thoughts for Quiet Days

by Elias Bloom · 2026-03-07 · ❤️ 34
#life #design #thoughts

Some days are not dramatic. They are simply quiet, pale, and a little unfinished. Those days need small rituals.

A glass of water. A soft playlist. A page half-written. A yellow object on the table that reminds you brightness can be subtle.

This story is for that mood — not sadness, not joy, just the clean middle where thinking becomes gentle.
