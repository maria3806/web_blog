Night Rain Notes

by Luna Mercer · 2026-03-07 · ❤️ 34
#mood #thoughts #art

The city becomes honest when it rains at night. Lights blur, edges soften, and suddenly every window looks like a quiet confession.

I wrote this piece for people who collect fragile moments — the ones that disappear if you try to explain them too quickly.

Maybe that is what lemoniq is for: a place where the atmosphere itself is allowed to speak.
