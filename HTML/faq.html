What is lemoniq? +
It's a creative platform for publishing unique stories under a lemon rain. We value deep meanings and minimalistic design.
How to become an author? +
Register through the main page and navigate to your dashboard. After logging in, you'll find a button to create new articles.
Why "lemoniq"? +
The name "lemoniq" combines "lemon" with "unique," reflecting our commitment to creating distinctive and refreshing content under the lemon rain.
Is it a final form of the site?+
No, it's still in development. In future, I want to add tags and filters to improve the user experience.
